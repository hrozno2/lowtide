# Audio credits

Three of the focus sounds (`storm`, `waves`, `garden`) blend a real field
recording under the synthesized layers — the recordings supply texture a
filter can't fake; the synthesis still supplies the macro-dynamics (the slow
swells and drift) and keeps everything else in the app sample-free. `cafe`
stays 100% synthesized on purpose: every real crowd recording we found still
carries occasional intelligible words, and looped for an hour that is a
"countable event" — exactly what this file's synthesis is built to avoid.

Each file below started life as a CC0 recording from freesound.org, trimmed
to a clean stretch and processed into a seamless loop with a short
(3-second) equal-power crossfade at the seam (see the `make_loop` step in
this project's history — plain `ffmpeg atrim`/`acrossfade`/`concat`, re-encoded
as low-bitrate Ogg Vorbis). CC0 dedications don't require attribution, but
it's credited here anyway for traceability.

<https://freesound.org/people/richwise/sounds/361401/>
